Common questions

What does a visit include?

We text you first, then walk your whole yard (front, back and sides) and pick up all the dog waste. We bag it, leave it in your trash can, and latch the gate when we leave.

How much does it cost?

Weekly service is $16.95 a visit for one dog on a standard yard. Each extra dog adds $3 a visit, and yard size moves the price a few dollars up or down. The price tool at the top shows your exact number.

Is the first visit really free?

Yes. Your first visit is free when you start any regular plan. No catch and no contract.

How does yard size change the price?

Prices assume a standard yard (1/8 to 1/4 acre). Small yards save $2 a visit. Large yards (1/4 to 1/2 acre) add $4, and extra large yards (1/2 to 1 acre) add $8. Over an acre, we give you a custom quote.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can reach the yard, we’ll handle it and close the gate on the way out. Leave your gate code in the sign-up form if you have one.

Do you scoop in winter?

All year, snow included. Your dog doesn’t take a season off, so neither do we.

Can you haul the waste away?

We bag it and leave it in your own trash can, so it goes out with your regular pickup.

How do I pay?

You’re billed once a month, at the start of the month, for the visits on your schedule. Pay by card, Venmo, Zelle or cash. Your free first visit is never billed.

How do I cancel or pause?

Text or call (952) 406-2121 anytime. No contract and no fees. Going on vacation? Text us and we’ll pause your visits.
